They replace today's letters in the HUD and the party list. A hero's effects also show as small symbols next to their name tag, instead of any words on the board. The weaker step of a damage-type effect is the same symbol drawn as an outline.

Text in the world

Name tags, damage and heal numbers, the Z and ! marks and the noises are Inter with a one-pixel black outline. A crit (Flanking Thrust from behind) is a larger gold number with no word. The E use hint becomes a small gold key cap. No effect words on the board: a hero's effects are symbols next to the name tag.

HUD

Inter for every label and number, with digits that keep their width. The bars, effect icons and ability slots get the brass rim. While an ability cools down, the slot's gold rim fills clockwise from the top, and the seconds left show in the corner. When the rim closes, the slot flashes once and glows gold until used.

Headings

IM Fell only where text is large: the boss name, the room messages, the rules log title and the menu title.
