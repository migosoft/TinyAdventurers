What to look for

A · Inter only

Clearest at small sizes and best for the dense sheet and tooltip texts. Neutral, so the adventure flavour has to come from the controls and the art.

B · Inter + Cinzel

Inter's readability for every text, Cinzel's carved Roman capitals for headings and names only. My pick: the flavour sits where it is cheap and the text stays easy to read.

C · Alegreya

A bookish, storybook feel in one family. Warmer than Inter and still readable, but the numbers in tight tables are a little less crisp.

D · Fell + Inter (chosen)

Old print headings, the most "ancient tome" mood. The headings are rougher and get hard to read below about 16 px, so they stay at heading sizes; every other text is Inter, in-game text too. Shown with Leather & brass and tighter button padding.
